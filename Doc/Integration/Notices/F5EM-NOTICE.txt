1. Scope of this mod
----------------------------------------------------------------------

This repository contains a community-developed DCS World aircraft mod
that models the Northrop F-5EM as operated by the Forca Aerea
Brasileira (FAB) after the Embraer / IAI Mectron modernization
program (2006-2013). The mod adds Brazilian-specific avionics, EW
profiles, weapon options, liveries, sounds and missions on top of
the publicly shipped F-5E-3 aircraft binary distributed by Eagle
Dynamics with DCS World.

This is a fan-made, non-commercial community project.

2. Third-party assets and attributions
----------------------------------------------------------------------

The mod uses the following third-party assets, redistributed under
the licenses granted by their original authors:

  - Base F-5E-3 aircraft binary, EFM and stock cockpit shape:
    Eagle Dynamics SA. Used as a runtime dependency only. Not
    redistributed in this repository.

  - Python-5 and Derby missile shapes (Shape/jas39_*.edm) and
    textures (textures/JAS_*.dds): JAS-39C Gripen Community Mod.
    Used with attribution per the original mod README.

  - DCS Lua scripting helpers (Cockpit/Scripts/DCS_LUA_functions.lua):
    Eagle Dynamics SA reference snippets. Marked as documentation
    only and excluded from the CI luac validation pass.

  - MinHook sources (ExternalFM/aar_hook_src/minhook/): Tsuda Kageyu
    and other identified contributors. These files retain their own
    BSD 2-Clause license in ExternalFM/aar_hook_src/minhook/LICENSE.txt.

  - avSimplest.dll and avSimplestWeaponSystem.dll: C++ systems from
    the A-29B Super Tucano Community Mod. Redistributed unmodified in
    this public, non-paid F-5EM mod with explicit permission granted
    on 2026-07-19 by Alexandre / alexandrelip, maintainer of the A-29B
    project, satisfying the A-29B README requirement that C++ reuse is
    allowed "upon request". The binaries remain the work of their
    original contributors; this NOTICE does not relicense them or
    expand the recorded permission. Release identity is pinned by
    SHA-256 in ci/release-binaries.psd1.

  - Sound effects and selected callouts: stock Eagle Dynamics
    libraries, plus Brazilian PT-BR voice samples contributed by
    community members (see Sounds/sdef/Aircrafts/F-5EM/AUTHORS.md
    when present).
